## The Problem It Answers

Never has entitlement been so high, yet responsibility so low.

A raw-balance vote would import that exact failure: it turns recognition into a **claim**, and the largest holder into a ruler. That is the opposite of what MEMES is for.

> Selection must read recognition **without inheriting plutocracy.**

<br>

## The Equilibrium of Vow

Selection weight is **not** a token balance. It is the meeting of two forces:

```
weight  =  f(recognized_contribution)  ×  active_vow
```

**`recognized_contribution`** — read from the Proof of Meaning ledger (`1.1.2`),
but **log-scaled**. Recognition earns a *seat*, never a throne. A contributor
recognized many times does not crush one recognized once.

**`active_vow`** — a renewable commitment a member stakes on a specific decision.
A vow **decays** if it is not renewed. Responsibility must be re-affirmed —
it is never inherited from past recognition.

> This is the equilibrium: the more you were recognized, the more vow you must
> post to keep your weight. Entitlement is taxed by responsibility.

## What Selection Is

Selection is not merely voting. It is the mechanism through which a decentralized
community **transforms intention into reality** — auditable, on-chain, neutral.

> The answer to a disagreement is not complaint.
> The answer is contribution: design a better mechanism, draft a #UIP, build a better process.

Selection is the process that lets those better mechanisms actually carry forward.

---

<br>

## How It Runs

1. **Read** — the Proof of Meaning ledger provides the recognized contributors and their weights.
2. **Weight** — each weight is log-scaled and multiplied by the member's active, non-decayed vow.
3. **Decide** — the weighted signal resolves the question; the outcome and every weight are public.
4. **Record** — the decision is written back as a contribution, so Selection itself stays auditable.

| Field | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| `decision_id` | Stable id for the selection |
| `question` | What was being decided |
| `ledger_ref` | PoM snapshot the weights were read from |
| `vow_total` | Sum of active vows staked |
| `outcome` | What carried forward |
| `tx_hash` | On-chain trace of the resolution |

Begin small and reversible, as the community suggested:

- First on a **MEME challenge** outcome — low stakes, fully public.
- Then on **VERSE initiatives** and community experiments.
- Only once the equilibrium holds there, graduate it to **#ToR-distribution** disputes and governance decisions.

> Prove the engine on toys before trusting it with the treasury.
