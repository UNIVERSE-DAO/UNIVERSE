# PROOF OF MEANING

_PoM v1 — Where Contribution Becomes Recognition_  
_The point where culture leaves a trace_

Proof of Meaning is the recognition layer of MEMES.  
Not a reward program — a way to make **respect visible**, and verifiable, on-chain.

> Memetics asks _what was meant_.  
> Proof of Meaning answers _that it was seen_.

---

<br>

## Position in the Lifecycle

A meme is born four times:

| Stage | Layer | Question |
|------|-------|----------|
| Intent | `1.1.1` Memetics | What is meant? |
| **Recognition** | **`1.1.2` Proof of Meaning** | **Was it seen?** |
| Structure | `1.1.3` Formatics | How is it formed? |
| Selection | `1.1.4` Voting | What carries forward? |

> PoM is the hinge: it turns intent into a record that Voting can act on.

---

<br>

## Why

PoM already rewards contribution — but _who contributed what, and received what_  
lives in memory and manual transfers.

For a protocol built on **public over private** and **verify over trust**,  
attribution should be as auditable as the token itself.

> Credible neutrality — not vibes — is what lets an aligned collective scale.

---

<br>

## What

A **public, append-only history of recognition**:  
each acknowledged contribution linked to its wallet, its moment, and its on-chain trace.

In the spirit of **#ToR — Token of Respect**, this is not a giveaway and not a promise.  
The value is never the amount. The value is the meaning made legible.

> No VC allocations. No private pools.  
> Every recognition originates from the same public supply available to everyone.

---

<br>

## How

### The Recognition Signal

A contribution is recognized when it is:

- **Public** — posted where the community can see, challenge, and learn  
- **Aligned** — it carries the protocol's intent, not just noise  
- **Voluntary** — offered, never extracted

### The Ledger

One record, read twice — by PoM for recognition, by `1.1.4` Voting for selection.

| Field | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| `contribution_id` | Stable id for the recognized contribution |
| `source` | Link to the post (TG / X) |
| `wallet` | Recipient address |
| `amount` | Tokens recognized |
| `tx_hash` | On-chain trace of the transfer |
| `date` | Timestamp of recognition |

> IDO pool → distributor → recipient.  
> Each hop public, each recognition traceable back to the moment that earned it.

The **visual representation** of this ledger lives as a dedicated PoM v1 section within  
[**DYOR**](https://universe.meme/#DYOR) — where the token is already proven safe.  
DYOR verifies the **asset**; PoM verifies the **distribution**. Two halves of one neutral system.
