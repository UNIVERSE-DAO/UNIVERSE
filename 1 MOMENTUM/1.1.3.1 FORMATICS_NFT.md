# FORMATICS_NFT (UIP_004)

Companion standard to the official #UIP_004 NFT Matching Machine (#NMM).

## WHY
In a world of infinite content, meaning becomes scarce.
NFTs introduced unique digital ownership - but ownership alone is not enough.
Ownership answers *who holds it*. It does not answer *why it matters*.
Proof of Meaning (1.1.2) already answers the second.
FORMATICS_NFT binds the two, so every NFT in the NMM carries its meaning, not only its owner.

## WHAT
An NFT in the UNIVERSE is not defined by its image.
It is defined by its MEANING - the mint-state of a recognized contribution.
Lifecycle: Memetics -> PoM -> Formatics -> Selection -> Mint.
One ledger, read again. Not a second registry.

## FIELD SCHEMA
| Field | Required | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `#NFT_<PILLAR>_<id>` | yes | CODEX pillar (VOW/GUIDE/COMMON/EQUILIBRIUM/SUCCESS/COMUD) + id |
| `#NFT_SUPPLY: <n>` | yes | edition size |
| `#PoM_REF: <contribution_id>` | on Proof | the recognized contribution this NFT mints |
| `$UNIVERSE` | yes | token anchor |
| `#uni_<x>_verse` | yes | single creator tag |

## NMM BINDING (Demand -> Supply -> Match -> Proof -> Success)
- **Demand / Supply** - permissionless. Anyone publishes a signal.
- **Match** - signals discover each other.
- **Proof** - MUST resolve to a `#PoM_REF`: the recognized `contribution_id` in the PoM ledger (1.1.2).
- **Success** - a Proof that is verified, not merely claimed.

Rule: an NFT without a `#PoM_REF` is ownership without meaning.
Demand and Supply stay open; only Proof must verify.
Freedom to create, accountability to claim.

## PRINCIPLE
verify > trust - extended from recognition to ownership.
Recognition is discovered, not claimed.
Verification over Forgery, made enforceable.

For the UNIVERSE.
